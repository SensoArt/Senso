RESonance

17–24 October 2026
Opening · 17 October · 17:30–21:30
NauART · Espronceda 154 · Barcelona

Paolo Agostini · Catherine Parra · Rafael Nuesi · Lucía Cheves Dauber
Curated by Claudia Alizieri

Where does the artwork end and our interpretation begin?

Looking is never a neutral act. A form remains after we stop looking at it. A colour alters the one we encounter next. Meaning is never fixed; it is constructed in what happens between the artwork and the person looking at it, making the boundary between the two uncertain.
