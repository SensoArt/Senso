Paolo Agostini · Catherine Parra · Rafael Nuesi · Lucía Cheves Dauber
Curated by Claudia Alizieri

Where does the artwork end and our interpretation begin?

Looking is never a neutral act.

Every form we recognise, every connection we imagine, every meaning we attribute arises from the encounter between what the artwork offers us and what we bring to it.

Resonances, contrasts and correspondences emerge between the works, progressively altering our perception.

Where do we end and the artwork begin?
