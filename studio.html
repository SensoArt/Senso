Rafael combines psychology, neuroscience, creativity and art to create experiences that foster personal and team development. Guatemalan artist based in Barcelona, Lucía explores painting as a form of memory and personal expression. Alongside her artistic practice, she teaches painting to both children and adults in Barcelona, creating spaces where experimentation, creativity and curiosity can unfold through art.
