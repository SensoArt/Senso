Arantxa Martinez Dugas

Arantxa Martinez Dugas is a multidisciplinary visual artist working across painting, fibers, drawing, sculpture, photography, video, and performance. Originally from Mexico and based in Montreal, her work explores themes such as the passage of time, space, perception, cultural identity, gender, and political art. She believes that as we live, we record and leave traces behind. Holding a BFA in Studio Arts from Concordia University and a DEC from LaSalle College, her research has been presented at ITSLIQUID Group / HUB ART Barcelona, The Holy Art / Mriya Gallery NY, BADA Mexico, and Art Toronto, along with an art residency at the Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity.
Arantxa Martinez Dugas es una artista visual multidisciplinaria que trabaja en pintura, fibras, dibujo, escultura, fotografía, video y performance. Originaria de México y radicada en Montreal, su obra explora temas como el paso del tiempo, el espacio, la percepción, la identidad cultural, el género y el arte político. Cree que a medida que vivimos, registramos y dejamos huellas. Licenciada en BFA Studio Arts por la Universidad Concordia y egresada del Colegio LaSalle, su investigación se ha presentado en ITSLIQUID Group / HUB ART Barcelona, The Holy Art / Mriya Gallery NY, BADA México y Art Toronto, además de una residencia artística en el Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity.
