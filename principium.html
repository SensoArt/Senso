PRINCIPIUM

Chapter I - May 2026 - Barcelona

In the beginning there was chaos

Before form, before order, before beauty, there was chaos. An indistinct, primordial space, charged with energy and possibility. Principium emerges from the idea of exploring the origin of everything, the moment when matter and emotion begin to take shape.

The exhibition brings together contemporary artists, each with a distinct and recognizable practice, whose works coexist within the space, creating an unstable balance of tensions and affinities.

Principium is an invitation to return to the beginning.

Antes de la forma, antes del orden, antes de la belleza, existía el caos. Un espacio indistinto, primordial, cargado de energía e posibilidad. Principium nace de la idea de explorar el origen de todas las cosas, el momento en el que la materia y la emoción comienzan a tomar forma.

La exposición reúne a artistas contemporáneos, cada uno con una poética autónoma y reconocible, que conviven en el espacio expositivo generando un equilibrio inestable de tensiones e afinidades.

Principium is an invitation to return to the beginning.
