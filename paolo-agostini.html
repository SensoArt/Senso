Paolo Agostini

Paul el Pulpo (Paolo Agostini) is an Italian self-taught abstract artist. Inspired by nature and introspection, his work explores freedom, connection, and the quiet rhythms of everyday life. His paintings emerge from an intuitive, physical process-working on horizontal canvases from multiple directions, he paints through an immersive practice guided by instinct. El Pulpo -his symbolic alter ego- represents curiosity, sensitivity, and adaptability, giving shape to his artistic identity: Paul el Pulpo. His work is rooted in the art of noticing: finding beauty in ordinary moments, being present, and remaining open to the wonder of life, a reminder that there is always something worth seeing when we take the time to look. Based between Barcelona and Cape Town, Paolo is a traveler and explorer, drawing inspiration from landscapes, cultures, people, and the spaces in between. His paintings have been exhibited in Barcelona, Berlin, Cape Town, and Tokyo.

Sus pinturas se han exhibido en Barcelona, Berlín, Ciudad del Cabo y Tokio.
