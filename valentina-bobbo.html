Valentina Bobbo

Valentina Bobbo is an Italian artist based in Barcelona, trained at the Brera Academy of Fine Arts and IED Milan. Working across installation, sculpture, video, and performance, her multidisciplinary practice incorporates salvaged materials to explore the transformation and adaptation of forms. She has participated in residencies including Can Serrat and Viafarini, presented solo exhibitions like 'Mostriciattola' and 'L’ora dell’oro', and joined group shows such as 'Good Girl' at MOMO Gallery (Athens) and 'Artefici del Nostro Tempo' for the Venice Biennale. She collaborates with AfterPoema Gallery, participating in Hybrid Art Fair (Madrid) and SWAB Art Fair (Barcelona).

Colabora con AfterPoema Gallery, participando en Hybrid Art Fair (Madrid) y SWAB Art Fair (Barcelona).
